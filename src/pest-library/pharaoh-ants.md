commonName: "Pharaoh Ants"
summary: "Tiny, pale yellow ants that nest indoors year-round and are notoriously difficult to eliminate with over-the-counter sprays."
appearance: "Pharaoh ants are very small, about 1.5–2 mm long, with a pale yellow to light brown body and a darker abdomen. Their small size and light colour make them easy to overlook until a trail is spotted along a baseboard or counter."
habitat: "Because they thrive in warm, humid conditions, pharaoh ants nest almost exclusively indoors in Ontario's climate — in wall voids, behind baseboards, under flooring, and near sources of heat and moisture such as furnaces and hot water pipes. A single colony can have multiple queens and satellite nests, allowing it to split and spread when disturbed."
risks:
  - "They are known to contaminate food and have been associated with spreading bacteria in sensitive environments like hospitals."
  - "Colonies can budd (split into new nests) if disturbed by improper treatment, making the problem worse."
  - "Their small size and habit of nesting deep in wall voids make DIY control largely ineffective."
preventionTips:
  - "Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers."
  - "Avoid using contact sprays on visible trails — this can trigger colony budding."
  - "Fix moisture issues, especially around heating equipment and plumbing."
  - "Contact a professional promptly, since pharaoh ant colonies rarely resolve on their own."
