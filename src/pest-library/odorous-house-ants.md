commonName: "Odorous House Ants"
summary: "Small dark brown to black ants best known for the distinct rotten-coconut smell they release when crushed."
appearance: "Odorous house ants are small, about 2.4–3.3 mm, and dark brown to black with an uneven, unevenly-shaped thorax when viewed from the side. Their most recognizable feature isn't visual — when crushed, they release a smell often described as similar to rotten coconut."
habitat: "These ants nest in a wide range of locations, both indoors and out — under stones, mulch, and logs outdoors, and in wall voids, near hot water pipes, and beneath flooring indoors. Colonies often move nest sites in response to rain or disturbance, which can make activity seem to appear suddenly."
risks:
  - "Large colonies can have multiple queens, making them persistent once established indoors."
  - "They forage widely for sugary foods and can contaminate counters and pantry items."
  - "Colonies often relocate after rain, which can bring outdoor activity indoors quickly."
preventionTips:
  - "Clean up sugary spills and store sweet foods in sealed containers."
  - "Trim vegetation and remove leaf litter or mulch piles touching the foundation."
  - "Seal gaps around utility lines, windows, and doors."
  - "Address moisture issues that may draw colonies indoors during wet weather."
