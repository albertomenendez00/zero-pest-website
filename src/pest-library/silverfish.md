commonName: "Silverfish"
summary: "Wingless, fish-shaped insects with a silvery, scaled appearance that thrive in damp areas and feed on starches found in paper, books, and fabric."
appearance: "Silverfish are small, wingless, and carrot-shaped, tapering from head to tail, with a silvery-grey, scaled body and three long, bristle-like tail filaments. They move in a quick, fish-like wriggling motion, which is how they get their name."
habitat: "They favour dark, humid environments — bathrooms, basements, laundry rooms, and areas around plumbing. They're often found among stored papers, books, cardboard boxes, and wallpaper, which provide both shelter and a food source in the starches and glues used in these materials."
risks:
  - "They can damage books, documents, wallpaper, and cardboard stored in damp areas."
  - "Their presence often signals a humidity problem worth addressing."
  - "Large populations in storage areas can go unnoticed until damage to stored items is discovered."
preventionTips:
  - "Reduce humidity in basements and bathrooms with a dehumidifier or improved ventilation."
  - "Store books, papers, and cardboard off the floor and away from damp walls."
  - "Fix leaks and address condensation around pipes and windows."
  - "Vacuum regularly in closets, basements, and storage areas to remove eggs and debris."
