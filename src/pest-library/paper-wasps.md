commonName: "Paper Wasps"
summary: "Slender wasps that build open, umbrella-shaped paper nests under eaves, railings, and other sheltered overhangs."
appearance: "Paper wasps have slender bodies, narrow waists, and long legs that dangle in flight, typically brown with yellow (and sometimes reddish) markings. They are generally less aggressive than yellowjackets or hornets unless their nest is directly disturbed."
habitat: "Nests are open-celled and umbrella-shaped, without an outer paper envelope, and are usually built in sheltered spots — under eaves, deck railings, porch ceilings, and in shrubs or overhangs. Colonies are started fresh each spring by a single overwintering queen and grow through the summer."
risks:
  - "Stings can cause pain and swelling, and multiple stings are possible if a nest is disturbed."
  - "Nests built near entrances, patios, or play areas create ongoing risk for occupants."
  - "Some individuals may have allergic reactions requiring medical attention."
preventionTips:
  - "Check eaves, railings, and overhangs early in the season for small, new nests."
  - "Seal gaps around soffits and rooflines that could shelter a nest."
  - "Keep outdoor garbage and food covered, particularly sweet drinks and proteins."
  - "Avoid disturbing an active nest — contact a professional for safe removal."
