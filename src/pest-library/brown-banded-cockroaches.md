summary: "Small cockroaches with light-coloured bands across the wings, known for hiding in dry areas away from water — including furniture and electronics."
appearance: "Brown-banded cockroaches are small, about 10–14 mm, light brown, with two lighter bands crossing the wings and abdomen (more visible in nymphs). Males have wings covering the full abdomen and can fly short distances; females have shorter wings."
habitat: "Unlike most cockroaches, brown-banded cockroaches don't need to stay near water and are often found in drier areas — inside furniture, behind picture frames, inside and behind appliances and electronics, and in closets and cupboards throughout a home, not just the kitchen."
risks:
  - "Their tendency to spread throughout a home, not just kitchens, can make infestations harder to locate."
  - "Like other cockroach species, allergens can affect indoor air quality and trigger allergy symptoms."
  - "Egg cases are often laid in hidden spots away from typical inspection areas, like inside electronics or furniture."
preventionTips:
  - "Reduce clutter throughout the home, including closets and storage areas, not just the kitchen."
  - "Vacuum and clean behind and under furniture and electronics periodically."
  - "Seal cracks around baseboards, outlets, and door and window frames."
  - "Have a professional inspect beyond the kitchen if activity is seen elsewhere in the home."
