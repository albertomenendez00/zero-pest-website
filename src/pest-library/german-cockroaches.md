commonName: "German Cockroaches"
summary: "Small, light brown cockroaches with two dark stripes behind the head — the most common cockroach found inside homes and restaurants."
appearance: "German cockroaches are light tan to brown, about 13–16 mm long, with two dark parallel stripes running down the area just behind the head. Nymphs are smaller and darker. They reproduce faster than most other cockroach species, which allows populations to grow quickly."
habitat: "They stay close to food, water, and warmth — commonly found in kitchens and bathrooms, behind appliances, inside cabinets, and near drains. They rarely travel far from a reliable food and moisture source, and egg cases (oothecae) are often tucked into cracks and crevices near where the colony is active."
risks:
  - "Allergens from cockroach droppings and shed skins can trigger asthma and allergy symptoms, especially in children."
  - "Rapid reproduction means small infestations can grow into large ones quickly if not addressed."
  - "They can spread bacteria as they move between drains, garbage, and food-prep surfaces."
preventionTips:
  - "Clean kitchens nightly, including under appliances and inside cabinets."
  - "Store food, including pet food, in sealed containers."
  - "Fix leaks and reduce standing moisture in kitchens and bathrooms."
  - "Reduce cardboard and paper clutter, which provides hiding and egg-laying sites."
