commonName: "Pavement Ants"
summary: "Small, dark brown to black ants that nest under pavement slabs, sidewalks, and foundations — one of the most common ants found in Ontario homes."
appearance: "Pavement ants are small, typically 2.5–4 mm long, and dark brown to blackish in colour with pale-coloured legs. Under magnification, parallel grooves are visible on the head and thorax. Winged swarmers may appear in spring or early summer."
habitat: "As their name suggests, pavement ants commonly nest under sidewalks, driveways, and slabs, as well as along foundation walls. Outdoors, look for small mounds of fine soil pushed up through cracks in pavement. Indoors, they often nest in wall voids, under floors, or near heat sources such as heating ducts."
risks:
  - "They contaminate food by foraging across kitchen counters, pantries, and garbage areas."
  - "Large outdoor colonies can undermine pavement and walkway edges over time."
  - "Indoor swarms of winged ants are often mistaken for a more serious infestation, causing unnecessary alarm."
preventionTips:
  - "Seal cracks in foundations, walkways, and slabs where ants can enter."
  - "Keep food in sealed containers and clean up crumbs and spills promptly."
  - "Fix moisture issues near foundations, as damp soil attracts colonies."
  - "Trim vegetation and remove debris from directly against exterior walls."
