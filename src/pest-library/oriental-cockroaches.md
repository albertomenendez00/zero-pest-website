summary: "Dark, shiny cockroaches sometimes called \"waterbugs,\" typically found in damp basements, drains, and crawlspaces."
appearance: "Oriental cockroaches are dark brown to almost black with a glossy body, about 25 mm long. Unlike American cockroaches, their wings are shortened and they are not capable of flight. They move more slowly than other common cockroach species."
habitat: "As their nickname \"waterbug\" suggests, they favour cool, damp, dark environments — basements, crawlspaces, floor drains, and areas around leaky pipes. Outdoors, they're often found under mulch, leaf litter, stones, and in damp woodpiles before moving indoors, especially in fall."
risks:
  - "Their preference for damp conditions often signals a moisture problem in the building."
  - "They can contaminate surfaces and food in basements or ground-floor storage areas."
  - "Odour from larger populations can become noticeable in enclosed, damp spaces."
preventionTips:
  - "Fix leaks and improve ventilation in basements and crawlspaces."
  - "Keep gutters and downspouts clear to direct water away from the foundation."
  - "Seal gaps around foundation walls, doors, and utility penetrations."
  - "Remove damp leaf litter, mulch, and wood debris from against exterior walls."
